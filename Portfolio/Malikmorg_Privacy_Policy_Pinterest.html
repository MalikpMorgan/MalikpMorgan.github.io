Privacy Policy

This policy covers the malikmorg personal, read-only Pinterest integration operated by Malik Morgan. It explains what Pinterest data the integration can access, why, and what it never does with that data.

At a glance — malikmorg

✓ Read-only access

It can view an authorized account, its boards, and its pins. It cannot create, edit, or delete anything on Pinterest.

✕ Never sold or shared

Pinterest data accessed through malikmorg is not sold, rented, used for advertising, or shared for marketing.

◐ You stay in control

Access only begins after you authorize it on Pinterest, and you can revoke it at any time in your Pinterest settings.

01Overview

malikmorg is the name used for this integration and for this privacy policy page. The integration (the “App”) connects to Pinterest through Pinterest’s official API so that its operator, Malik Morgan (Pinterest: @malikmorgan16), can view and work with Pinterest content he has authorized access to — for example, reviewing boards and pins he has saved for a creative project.

The App is a personal tool operated under the name malikmorg. It is not a public product, it does not offer accounts to the general public, and it does not provide a service to third parties in exchange for their Pinterest data.

By authorizing the malikmorg App on Pinterest, you agree to the practices described here for the data Pinterest makes available through that authorization.

02Information accessed through Pinterest

The malikmorg App requests only the following read-only Pinterest API permissions (scopes). Nothing beyond these scopes is requested:

user_accounts:read
Basic account information Pinterest provides for the authorized account, such as username, display name, profile image, and account type.
boards:read
Boards belonging to or accessible by the authorized account — including board name, description, privacy setting, and pin counts.
pins:read
Pins on those boards — including pin title, description, image, destination link, and the board each pin belongs to.

Data is retrieved from Pinterest only when the operator makes a request that needs it — for example, asking to list boards or to review the pins on a specific board. The App does not continuously monitor a Pinterest account in the background.

03What is not collected

  • No Pinterest password. Sign-in and authorization happen entirely on Pinterest. The App never sees or stores your Pinterest password.
  • No write access. The App has no permission to create, edit, move, or delete boards or pins, and it does not request those permissions.
  • No messages or private communications from Pinterest, and no payment, billing, or financial information.
  • No data from people who have not authorized the App, beyond Pinterest content that is already public.
  • No location data, contacts, or files from your device in connection with Pinterest access.

04How information is used

Information accessed through Pinterest is used solely to carry out the operator’s own requests, including:

  • Displaying and reviewing the authorized account’s boards and pins;
  • Searching, organizing, and referencing saved pins — for example, gathering visual references for a video game / story idea;
  • Confirming that the connection to Pinterest is working correctly.

Not used for: advertising, profiling, building audiences, training models for third parties, credit or eligibility decisions, or any form of automated marketing.

05How information is shared

It isn’t, in the ordinary course of use. Pinterest data accessed through malikmorg is:

  • Never sold or rented to any third party;
  • Never shared for advertising or marketing purposes;
  • Never published as someone else’s private content. Public board and pin content remains attributed to its Pinterest source.

The only exceptions are narrow: data may be disclosed if required by law, a valid court order, or to protect the rights and safety of the operator or others. Pinterest itself processes data under Pinterest’s own Privacy Policy when the API is used.

06Data storage & retention

  • Access credentials (OAuth access and refresh tokens issued by Pinterest) are held in secure credential storage provided by the platform hosting the App — not in this page, and not in plain text in chat or documents.
  • Board and pin data is fetched on demand to answer a request. It is not built into a permanent, separate database for resale or long-term profiling.
  • Retention: any working copies created while fulfilling a request are kept only as long as they are useful for that task. When access is revoked or the App is disconnected, its tokens are no longer usable and should be deleted from credential storage.

07Security

Access uses Pinterest’s OAuth 2.0 authorization flow over HTTPS. Reasonable measures are taken to protect tokens and any data in transit, including keeping credentials out of public code, pages, and messages.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. If you believe your Pinterest account or an authorization has been compromised, change your Pinterest password and revoke the App’s access immediately (see the next section).

08Your choices & revoking access

Authorization is optional and revocable. You can withdraw the malikmorg App’s access at any time:

  • In Pinterest, go to your account Settings and review the apps / permissions connected to your account, then remove this App; or
  • Disconnect the Pinterest connection in the tool where you connected it.

Once revoked, the App can no longer retrieve your account, boards, or pins through the API. You may also contact the operator (below) to ask what, if anything, is retained and to request its deletion.

If you authorized this App and would like your accessed data deleted, contact the operator with your Pinterest username and the request will be honored promptly.

09Cookies & tracking on this page

This malikmorg privacy policy page itself sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and contains no advertising trackers. It is a static informational page.

10Children’s privacy

The App is a personal tool for the operator’s own Pinterest account and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly access or collect personal information from children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction).

11Changes to this policy

If the App’s permissions or practices change — for example, if write access were ever requested — this page will be updated first, the “Last updated” date at the top will change, and new Pinterest authorization would be required before any expanded access takes effect.
